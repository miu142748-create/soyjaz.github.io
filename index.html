✨ Dissertation Collection ✨

jazzie's dissertation collection examines the history of border enforcement and state violence, starting with the 1917 Bath Riots. It also looks at why traditional school and university systems need a critical update, and explores how community zines and mutual aid sustain grassroots resistance.
