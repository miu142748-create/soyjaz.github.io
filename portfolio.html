Innovating Online Learning in Technical and Professional Communication

The Goal: Engineered an end-to-end asynchronous learning module using the ADDIE framework to fix navigation bottlenecks and modernize digital literacy training in D2L Brightspace.

Frameworks & Tools: D2L Brightspace LMS, ADDIE Model, Multimodal UDL Strategies

Needs and Learner Analysis

Overview: Evaluated student entry-level competencies and LMS interaction metrics to restructure Technical & Professional Communication into an accessible General Education format.

Target Population: Undergraduate general education students from diverse non-technical disciplines, prioritizing multimodal support and plain-language guidance.

Check-In Design

The Overview: Designed low-stakes formative evaluation checkpoints to track learner comprehension, maintain engagement, and give instructors real-time feedback throughout the course module.

Features: Structured self-reflection prompts, bite-sized assessment loops, and accessible submission formats designed to reduce cognitive load while keeping students on track.

Usability Testing Video

The Test: Put real users in the driver's seat to stress-test the module's layout, cut out friction points, and make sure the content actually flows for a Professional & Technical Writing audience.

What it is: Usability testing is a hands-on evaluation method used to see how intuitively users can navigate a digital environment without getting lost or overwhelmed.

Breakdown:

  • Live observation of users completing core tasks inside D2L Brightspace.
  • Pinpointing clunky menus, hidden instructions, or broken design loops.
  • Iterating on user feedback to streamline the overall experience.
